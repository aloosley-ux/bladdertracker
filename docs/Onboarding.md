# Onboarding Guide

## Welcome

Welcome to **BladderTracker**, an app designed for UK families, caregivers, educators, and clinicians. It helps you track continence, daily routines, and child development in one calm, mobile-first diary so you can identify patterns, share insights with your care team, and support your child&apos;s wellbeing.

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to the app and select **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a strong password.
3. Select your role (Parent, Caregiver, School Admin, etc.).
4. Your role determines what you can see and do within the app. For example, a Parent has full access to their child's data, while a Caregiver may have limited permissions.

## Adding Your First Child

1. Navigate to **Profiles** from the navigation bar.
2. Click **Add child**.
3. Enter the child's name and date of birth.
4. The child profile is now ready for tracking. You can start logging entries straight away.

## Understanding the Dashboard

- The **Dashboard** shows today's overview for your selected child.
- **Quick-add buttons** let you log entries quickly without navigating away.
- **Summary cards** show daily totals across all modules at a glance.
- **Recent entries** are displayed below the summary cards so you can review what has been logged.

## Logging Entries

1. Use the **quick-add buttons** on the Dashboard, or navigate to **Add Entry** from the menu.
2. Select from the available modules:
   - **Drinks** – Track fluid intake.
   - **Urine** – Record urine output.
   - **Bowel** – Log bowel movements.
   - **Sleep** – Track sleep duration and quality.
   - **Toilet Attempts** – Record toilet training attempts.
   - **Food** – Log meals and snacks.
   - **Mood** – Track emotional state throughout the day.
   - **Sensory** – Record sensory experiences and responses.
   - **Medication** – Log administered medications.
   - **Therapy** – Track therapy sessions.
   - **Routine** – Record daily routine activities.
3. Each module has specific fields relevant to that tracker. Fill in the required information.
4. Add **notes** to provide additional context for any entry.

## Viewing Your Log

1. Navigate to **Log** to see all recorded entries.
2. Use the **calendar strip** to select different dates and view past records.
3. **Filter by module type** to find specific entries quickly.
4. **Delete entries** that are no longer needed by selecting the entry and confirming removal.

## Reports & Trends

1. Navigate to **Reports** to see patterns and trends over time.
2. Select a **time period** — 7, 14, or 30 days — to adjust the reporting window.
3. **Toggle different event types** on or off to focus on the data that matters most.
4. Use the **charts** to identify patterns and share insights with your care team.

## Managing Modules

1. Go to **Settings** and find the **module management** section.
2. **Toggle modules on or off** for each child based on what you need to track.
3. Only **enabled modules** appear on the Dashboard and in the Add Entry screen.
4. Changes apply instantly, so you can return to the Dashboard and start logging straight away.

## Inviting Caregivers

1. Navigate to **Profiles**.
2. Create an **invite** by entering the caregiver's email address and assigning a role.
3. **Share the invite link** with the caregiver.
4. Once accepted, caregivers can log entries for your child based on the permissions you have granted.

## Data & Privacy

- Your data is **stored securely** and handled in accordance with UK data protection regulations.
- **Export your data** as CSV from **Settings > Data & Privacy**.
- **Delete your account** and all associated data at any time from the same section.
- View the full **GDPR policy** from Settings for detailed information on how your data is processed.

## Accessibility

- Switch between **Light**, **Dark**, and **High Contrast** themes in Settings.
- The app supports full **keyboard navigation**.
- The interface is **screen reader compatible**.
- **Clear, simple language** is used throughout the app to ensure ease of use for all users.

## Getting Help

- Visit the **Help & Support** page for FAQs and guidance on using the app.
- Contact your **care team or administrator** for additional support with your child's tracking needs.
